Top 50 Skate Video Soundtracks

Top artists

Video tracklists come from SkateVideoSite, pulled October 10, 2026. The Tony Hawk's Pro Skater soundtracks come from the Tony Hawk's Games Wiki (1 and 2) and GameGrin (3). A question mark means the song is not identified in the source. Artist names are standardized so each artist is spelled one way. Spotify and YouTube links open a search for that artist and song, and each video has a YouTube search link for the video itself. Top track is the song in each video with the most Last.fm listeners, leaving out the closing song (CKY2K's top track is an SSD crew pick), and Top 5 per video ranks songs by Last.fm listeners.

411VM is 411 Video Magazine, the quarterly skate video magazine that ran 67 issues from 1993 to 2005. Lance Mountain hosted the first 50. CKY is Camp Kill Yourself, the Bam Margera video series and the band named for it. Nike SB is Nike Skateboarding. THPS is Tony Hawk's Pro Skater. SSD is Smoke Skate Drink, the crew behind Cliché.

The ranking is a judgment based on influence and how often each video is named in skate media retrospectives, with the top 10 set by the SSD crew, who came up skating in the early 2000s. The Tony Hawk's Pro Skater games and Cliché sit outside the ranking. No sales figures exist for these videos.
